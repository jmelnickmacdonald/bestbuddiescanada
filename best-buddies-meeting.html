WHAT BEST BUDDIES IS ALL ABOUT

Friendship is
where it starts.

Best Buddies Canada brings students with and without intellectual and developmental disabilities (IDD) together to build genuine friendships and more inclusive school communities.

At its heart, the program is about reciprocal friendships, not assigning some students to help others.

Free for schools. Flexible by design. Supported every step of the way.

A PROGRAM THAT FITS YOUR SCHOOL

Make Best Buddies your own.

There's no single way to run a chapter. The best approach is one that reflects your students, their interests and your school community.

Group Gatherings

A relaxed lunch, games, music, projects or shared activities once or twice a month.

One-to-One Friendships

Buddy pairs based on shared interests and mutual preferences, with guidance and ongoing support. Students can build friendships naturally through shared activities, smaller groups and regular opportunities to connect.

02

Student-Led Chapters

A Chapter President or executive team helps plan gatherings, welcome members and lead initiatives. Students help choose activities, welcome members, plan projects and contribute their ideas.

SHARED RESPONSIBILITY

You don't have to do it all.

School staff guide the chapter, students help bring it to life, and Best Buddies Canada provides ongoing support.

Staff Advisor

Provides guidance and supervision, supports recruitment and registration, helps plan gatherings and supports buddy pairings where applicable.

Student Members

Build friendships, share interests, participate in activities and help shape the chapter. Student executives can coordinate planning, events and recruitment. Students can take on age-appropriate leadership responsibilities.

Program Advisor

Helps with setup, planning, training, recruitment, accessibility, friendship support and any questions along the way.

Your chapter. Your students. Your way of making it work.
