About Best Buddies

Friendship is where it starts.

Best Buddies brings students with and without intellectual and developmental disabilities (IDD) together to build genuine friendships, discover shared interests and contribute to their school community.

1989

Best Buddies began through Anthony Kennedy Shriver. Best Buddies Canada launched in 1993. Today, chapters bring students together in schools and communities across Canada.

The best friendships grow through shared interests, mutual choice and time together. Everyone brings something to the friendship.

Free for schools. Flexible by design. Supported every step of the way.

Building on what's already working

Your school may already have excellent clubs, leadership opportunities or inclusive activities. Best Buddies doesn't need to replace them.

Keep what works.

Become part of something bigger.

The difference?

Best Buddies brings an intentional focus on ongoing friendships, with the structure, community and support to help those connections grow.

Your chapter, your way

Make Best Buddies your own.

There's no single way to run a chapter. Find an approach that makes sense for your students, schedule and school community.

Group Gatherings

Meet over lunch, play games, create art, listen to music or work on a project together. Regular gatherings give students opportunities to discover shared interests and build friendships.

An easy, welcoming place to begin

One-to-One Friendships

Students can be paired based on shared interests and mutual preferences. Buddy pairs spend time getting to know one another, with guidance from their Staff Advisor and Best Buddies Canada.

Friendships can develop through shared activities, smaller groups and regular time together. Schools can also explore structured buddy relationships where appropriate.

Connections built through shared interests

Student-Led & Hybrid

Student leaders can plan gatherings, welcome members and organize events. Chapters can combine buddy friendships, group activities and a Chapter President or executive team.

Younger students can choose activities, welcome members and help plan projects. Middle school students may take on more leadership responsibilities as they grow.

Making it manageable

Start with what works. Grow from there.

A chapter can fit naturally into existing school routines, with responsibilities shared among staff and students.

Once a month?

A predictable lunchtime gathering can be a wonderful starting point. Choose a schedule your school can comfortably sustain.

Already have a club?

Best Buddies might fit within a lunch program, social group, leadership class or another established school activity.

Busy staff?

Share planning with colleagues and students. Our activity resources help take the guesswork out of preparing gatherings.

Only a few students?

Start with a smaller group and invite interested students to join over time. Chapter size doesn't determine its impact.

Limited supplies?

Use games, craft materials, music and spaces you already have. Many of the simplest activities work beautifully.

Ready for more?

Students can eventually explore outings, leadership roles, buddy friendships and school-wide projects.

The Staff Advisor's role: Provide guidance and supervision, support recruitment and registration, and help students organize activities and buddy friendships where offered. Best Buddies Canada provides resources and ongoing support.

Students can lead the way.

Elementary and middle school students can welcome members, plan activities, create seasonal decorations and contribute to projects that make their school more welcoming.

Student leaders can organize events, recruitment and school-wide initiatives. Chapters may work with student council or leadership classes, or have their own Chapter President and executive team.

Becoming part of Best Buddies

A few simple steps. A whole community behind you.

1
Connect your school

Confirm principal approval, identify a Staff Advisor and complete the school's Program Application Form.

2
Make your chapter your own

Choose an approach, meeting time and location that work for your students. Existing programs can often be incorporated.

3
Welcome your members

We'll provide a school-specific registration link and guide you through student registration, consent and safeguarding requirements.

4
Get everyone ready

We'll help coordinate the required training and share recruitment materials, activity ideas and practical resources.

5
Start building friendships

Enjoy your first gathering, welcome student ideas and begin connecting with the wider Best Buddies community.

Registration helps establish your chapter and ensures students can participate appropriately and safely. We'll guide you through the process.
