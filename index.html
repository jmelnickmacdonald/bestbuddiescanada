Why Best Buddies matters

Every student deserves to feel known, included, and part of school life.

Best Buddies brings together students with and without intellectual and developmental disabilities through friendship clubs, one-to-one friendships, group activities, and student leadership.

Students spend time together and get to know one another beyond the roles they may usually have at school. For students with intellectual and developmental disabilities, that can mean more opportunities for friendship and social connection. Students build new relationships, gain new perspectives, and have opportunities to lead.

Friendship

Regular opportunities to spend time together, discover shared interests, and build relationships.

Belonging

More ways for students to feel welcomed, recognized, and part of school life.

Leadership

Students can help plan, welcome others, take responsibility, and shape the culture around them.

Built to fit your school

A flexible program that can take shape around your school.

Best Buddies does not have to look exactly the same everywhere. Chapters can reflect the age of the students, the school schedule, student interests, and the people involved.

Elementary & Middle School

Friendship starts with time together.

Students come together through games, activities, lunch, conversation, and shared experiences. Chapters are group-focused, social, and supported by school staff and Best Buddies Canada.

Quick answers

A few things schools often ask.

The practical pieces are simple. Best Buddies is free for participating schools, fundraising is optional, and chapters are designed to fit around the school day.

Explore BestBuddies.ca Helpful resources Email Julianna

Is there a cost?

No. Best Buddies school programs are free for participating schools.

Is fundraising required?

No. Fundraising is optional. If your chapter chooses to participate, including in a Friendship Walk, Best Buddies provides resources and support.

How much time does it take?

Chapters are designed to work around the school’s schedule, with regular meeting times that make sense for students and staff.

Will we have support?

Yes. Best Buddies provides training, practical resources, activity ideas, planning guidance, and ongoing staff support throughout the year.

Support for educators

You have support along the way.

We know school days are already full. Best Buddies provides practical tools, training, activity ideas, fundraising and Friendship Walk resources, and guidance throughout the year.

We’re also here for the day-to-day questions that come with running a chapter — from planning activities and supporting student leaders to figuring out what works best in your school.
