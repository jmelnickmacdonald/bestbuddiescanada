What Best Buddies looks like

Connection looks different for everyone.

A Best Buddies chapter creates more ways to meet people, spend time together, find common ground, and feel part of campus and community life.

Friendship

One-to-one connections built around shared interests, conversation, outings, and time together.

Community

Group activities, campus events, games, celebrations, coffee, outings, and whatever the chapter enjoys doing together.

Belonging

A chapter culture where people are known, included, and encouraged to contribute in their own way.

Chapters can use one-to-one friendships, group activities, or a mix of both. The program can take shape around the people involved.
Make it yours

There’s more than one way to lead.

Best Buddies chapters are student-led. Some people want to steer the whole chapter. Others would rather own one part of it. Build an executive around the people and strengths you have.

Lead the chapter

Set direction, keep the team moving, and help turn ideas into action.

Plan the fun

Shape activities, gatherings, outings, and events people actually want to attend.

Bring people in

Recruit new members, welcome people, and help everyone find a way to take part.

Tell the story

Handle social media, communications, promotion, and chapter visibility around campus.

Build support

Help with fundraising, campus connections, partnerships, and the practical pieces that keep things moving.

Keep it growing

Build the next group of leaders so the chapter stays strong after people graduate.

Skills you’ll actually use: Leadership Communication Project planning Recruitment Event coordination Relationship building Problem solving
