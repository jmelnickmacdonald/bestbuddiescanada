What Best Buddies looks like

Connection looks different for everyone.

A Best Buddies chapter creates more ways to meet people, spend time together, find common ground, and feel part of campus and community life.

Friendship

One-to-one connections built around shared interests, conversation, outings, and time together.

Community

Group activities, campus events, games, celebrations, coffee, outings, and whatever the chapter enjoys doing together.

Belonging

A chapter culture where people are known, included, and encouraged to contribute in their own way.

Chapters can use one-to-one friendships, group activities, or a mix of both. The program can take shape around the people involved.
Make it yours

There’s more than one way to lead.

You don’t have to be the person doing everything. Strong chapters work because different people take on different pieces: leading the team, planning activities, welcoming members, communications, fundraising, and helping the chapter carry forward.

Lead the chapter

Set direction and keep things moving.

Plan the fun

Shape activities, outings, and events.

Bring people in

Recruit, welcome, and help members find their place.

Tell the story

Handle social media, promotion, and campus visibility.

Build support

Help with fundraising and campus connections.

Keep it growing

Help the next student leaders take over.

Along the way: leadership · communication · project planning · recruitment · event coordination · relationship building · problem solving
Useful beyond campus

What you build here goes with you.

Whether you’re heading into education, healthcare, social work, psychology, recreation, business, communications, or something completely different, Best Buddies gives you real experience working with people, leading a team, solving problems, and creating spaces where more people can participate.

Friendship comes first. The perspective and skills you build come with you.
