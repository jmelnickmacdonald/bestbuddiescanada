Best Buddies Canada

Ottawa expansion

I’ve started mapping out a few different ways to grow Best Buddies in Ottawa. Some of this is already underway, and some is still exploratory. I’d really value your perspective on the local landscape and where you think the best opportunities may be.

Current thinking October visit Your input

Current thinking

1

School boards

I’ve started outreach with OCDSB and OCSB, with the goal of building relationships at the central level before doing a lot of school-by-school outreach.

  • Identify schools that may already be a good fit
  • Get warm introductions where possible
  • Look for opportunities to speak to larger groups of staff or students
2

Schools + current chapters

I want a clearer picture of what is already working locally, where chapters may have gone quiet, and which schools could provide good examples for new conversations.

  • Reconnect with quieter chapters where it makes sense
  • Identify a few strong local chapters
  • Build a targeted list of possible new schools
3

Parents + community

I’d like to build more awareness outside the school boards and better understand where families are already interested in seeing something like Best Buddies.

  • OCDSB and OCSB SEAC
  • Parent involvement groups and school councils
  • Ottawa inclusion and disability organizations
4

Student + educator networks

I’m also interested in groups that can help us reach more than one school at a time.

  • Student Senate / student leadership
  • Learning Support and Special Education teams
  • Student Success and guidance staff
  • Principal / vice-principal networks
5

Post-secondary

I want the university and college side to grow alongside the school program.

  • uOttawa: reconnect with the current chapter and make sure there is strong leadership coming behind the current executive
  • Carleton: explore starting a chapter
  • Algonquin: explore starting a chapter
6

Eastern Ontario

I also have some useful relationships in CDSBEO, where Best Buddies already has a number of chapters.

  • Student Senate
  • Parent engagement
  • School leadership
  • Additional schools that may already be a natural fit
I’m deliberately not starting with a mass email to every school. I’d rather use board relationships, local chapters, families, and student leaders to make as many of those conversations warm as possible.
